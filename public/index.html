    A LITTLE FRIENDLY COMPETITION

    The rules of our date

    Cherry (Dylan) goes first. Move diagonally forward on the dark squares. Drag a piece, or select a piece and then its destination. Use Hint if you want legal destinations highlighted.

    Jumps are required. Jump over an opponent to capture them. Keep jumping with the same piece while another capture is available.

    Reach the far edge to earn a crown. Kings move and capture diagonally in both directions. Crowning ends your turn. You can select any of your pieces, but when a capture is available you must capture. After the first jump, finish that piece’s remaining jumps.

    Win by capturing every opponent or leaving them without a move. 80 moves without a capture or crown is a draw.

    Online: choose your color when creating a room. The other player gets the opposite color. Only your own pieces can move on your turn. Rooms remain available while a player is online; an inactive room expires after 24 hours. A new game online needs both players’ agreement.
